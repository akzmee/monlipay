MonliPay: Send tokens with a link
Track: Consumer Products & Payments

Pitch draft for the founder to record, under two minutes. Add your real name, background and reason for building it. The initial segment below is a hypothesis until confirmed by interviews or user testing.

Imagine a community organizer sending a reward to ten members after an event. Today, that can mean collecting ten wallet addresses, making separate transfers, and checking who received the money.

MonliPay starts with a funded link. The organizer chooses a token, a total amount and the number of recipients, then shares the link in the group's existing chat. A pool of 100 USDC for ten recipients gives each wallet 10 USDC. The contract holds the funds, enforces the quota, prevents repeat claims from the same wallet and returns unclaimed funds after expiry. Organizers can restrict eligibility to selected wallets or open the link to anyone. Random shares are optional.

The working product also supports individual Payment Links, saved contacts, encrypted link history and an optional private payment mode. We built on an existing payment-link project and disclose that baseline. This hackathon's work adds group distributions, the unified interface, encrypted records and the private-payment integration.

Equal Group Payment is deployed on Monad mainnet and testnet. Our testnet checks exercised real token settlement and expiry refunds. Automated checks do not establish consumer adoption. Our next step is to test the full flow with community organizers and recipients, measure where people need help, and remove wallet and gas friction.
